description: The 2q37 copy number variation syndrome can result in the loss of up
  to 78 protein-coding genes. Patients with 2q37 CNV syndrome have intellectual disability,
  facial dysmorphism and skeletal and digit malformations (Sakai et al., 2014; López-Uriarte
  et al., 2013). Research linked changes in HDAC4 gene with obesity of 2q37 patients
  (López-Uriarte et al., 2013). Chromosomal locations are from Kirov et al. 2014,
  and the larger one from Sakai et al., 2014.
